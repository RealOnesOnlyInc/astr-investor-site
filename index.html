THE FOUNDING INSIGHT

Don’t manufacture community.
Activate the one already there.

Gyms already bring members, trainers, and staff into the same physical space. Sharing a membership, though, doesn’t automatically create a relationship.

Our go-to-market hypothesis is to activate that concentrated audience location by location—not acquire isolated app users across an entire city.

Events create shared experiences. Afterglow helps people reconnect. Consent-based attendance and engagement signals help operators understand what brings people back.

THE DISTRIBUTION WEDGE

Win the gym.
Earn the network.

The existing gym membership is our starting audience. The trainer and staff relationships are the trust layer. Recurring experiences are the activation engine. We test density in one location before expanding to the next.

THE MARKET-ENTRY PRINCIPLE

Local density before broad reach. A community with meaningful repeat interactions can be more valuable than a city of disconnected downloads.

  1. 01 / EMBED

    Start with the operator.

    Partner with a gym or studio that already has members, trainers, staff, and an established meeting place.

  2. 02 / ACTIVATE

    Make relationships happen.

    Verified onboarding, trainer-led events, QR check-ins, and Afterglow turn proximity into recurring connections.

  3. 03 / REPLICATE

    Scale what repeats.

    Measure activation, connection, return visits, and operator value. Refine the playbook before moving to another location.

Our proposed advantage is embedded access to recurring communities—not exclusive ownership of them.

Why now: Fitness experiences are becoming social products. Our opportunity is to validate a gym-embedded distribution model in Canadian communities while the category is still taking shape. Competitive timing and network effects are strategic hypotheses—not claimed pilot results.

PRODUCT

The social layer inside the gym.

ASTR complements existing gym membership, access, and billing systems. Our wedge is the layer between members, trainers, staff, and real-world sessions: identity, events, connections, and operator engagement insights.

THE SECOND DISTRIBUTION ENGINE

Gyms seed the network. Hosts keep it moving.

Trainers, instructors, and run-club leaders are natural community connectors. Inside partner locations, their recurring sessions create reasons to participate. Outside the gym, their audiences can extend reach. Paid experiences are a future monetization test—not a requirement for the first pilot.

BEACHHEAD

Targeting a first Winnipeg gym pilot: activate its existing membership base through trainers and recurring sessions, then observe verified onboarding, RSVP-to-attendance conversion, Afterglow connections, and 30-day repeat participation. Partner results have not yet been established.
